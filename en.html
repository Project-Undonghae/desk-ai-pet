HOW IT WORKS

01
Download & install

Grab the build for your platform above and run it. You can enable launch at login if you like.

02
Connect your AI

Pick Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini in settings and add your API key. Stored in the OS keychain.

03
Open with a shortcut

Ctrl+Shift+Space (Windows) / Cmd+Shift+Space (macOS) opens the chat anywhere.
